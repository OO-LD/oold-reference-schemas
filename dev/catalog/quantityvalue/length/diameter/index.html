Diameter¶

A Length taken across a round object through its centre. It exists to show that the chain continues: a subschema of a subschema inherits both sets of terms and both sets of mappings.

  • extends: Length (allOf), which extends QuantityValue
  • module: quantities
  • published at quantities/1.0/Diameter.schema.json

Note how little it declares. The units, their aliases, the value and uncertainty terms and every mapping on them come from Length and QuantityValue; Diameter only narrows the class term and the example. That is the point of the chain: a new quantity costs a handful of lines, and it inherits a validated mapping set rather than restating one.

Alternative readings¶

  • QUDT quantitykind:Diameter is the consensus reading.
  • EMMO Diameter (EMMO_c1c8ac3c..., ISO 80000 item 3-1.5) is an exact match.
  • PATO diameter (PATO_0001334) is a close match for the same reason as Length: PATO models it as a length quality of a bearer rather than as a value with a unit.
