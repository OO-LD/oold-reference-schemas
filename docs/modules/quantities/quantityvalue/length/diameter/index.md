# Diameter

`Length` taken across a round object through its centre. It narrows the class term and the
example, and inherits its units, terms and mappings from `Length` and `QuantityValue`.

## On the mappings

QUDT `quantitykind:Diameter` is the consensus reading and EMMO `Diameter` (ISO 80000 item
3-1.5) an exact match. PATO `diameter` is a close match only, for the same reason as
[Length](../): PATO models it as a length quality of a bearer rather than as a value with a
unit.
